Frequently Asked Questions

Google Play Console par app publish karne me kitna time lagta hai?

Generally 3 to 7 days for review. If testing is required, it adds 14 days.

What is Google's new 20-tester rule?

New personal accounts must have 20 people test their app for 14 continuous days before publishing.

Kya mera app suspend hone ka khatra hai?

If you follow Google's policies and use original code/assets, your app is completely safe.

Agar app update karna ho toh kya charge hoga?

We offer free updates within a specific period depending on your chosen plan. Minimal charges apply later.
